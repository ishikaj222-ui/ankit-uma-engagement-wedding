2. Scratch Animation – Wedding Date Only

Create ONLY ONE scratch-to-reveal interaction on the entire website.

Place it after the main wedding invitation cover and before the wedding countdown.

Heading:
"A Special Date Awaits You!"

Description:
"Scratch the golden surface to discover when our forever begins."

Scratch card design:

Antique gold scratch surface.

Delicate floral corners.

Elegant Indian wedding ornaments.

Small golden heart or decorative question mark.

Rounded rectangular card.

Hidden content underneath:

SAVE THE DATE

28 NOVEMBER 2026

SATURDAY

ANANYA & SAMARTH

"Two hearts, one beautiful journey, forever together."

Interaction:

Visitors can scratch using their fingers on mobile.

Support touch and mouse interactions.

Reveal the date gradually.

Once approximately 50% of the card is scratched, automatically reveal the remaining content.

Add subtle golden sparkles and flower petals when the date is revealed.

IMPORTANT: Do not add scratch animations to Haldi, Mehendi, Sangeet, or individual event cards.

3. Wedding Countdown

Display a live countdown to 28 November 2026 at 7:00 PM.

Show:

Days

Hours

Minutes

Seconds

Use elegant golden countdown boxes with subtle floral detailing.

The countdown should update automatically every second.

4. Four Individual Wedding Event Cards

Display four vertically stacked event cards in chronological order.

Each card must contain:

Event illustration.

Event title.

Full date.

Day of the week.

Starting and ending time.

Venue name.

Full address.

City and state.

View Location button.

Use a unique visual theme for each celebration:

Haldi: Yellow, orange, marigold flowers and turmeric decorations.

Mehendi: Emerald green, pastel green, floral arches and henna-inspired patterns.

Sangeet: Magenta, royal purple, antique gold, lanterns and musical decorations.

Wedding: Royal red, maroon, champagne gold, floral mandap and traditional Indian wedding decorations.

All event dates and details must be visible directly on their cards. No scratch interactions on these cards.

5. Final Important Requirements

Build the entire experience exclusively for mobile screens.

Use a 375px–430px portrait-first design.

No desktop layout or desktop navigation.

Start with two interactive palace doors that open on tapping.

Use the attached images as visual design references.

Keep all sections in one continuous mobile scrolling experience.

Add realistic dummy content everywhere.

Never show empty fields, Lorem Ipsum, TBD, or bracketed placeholders.

Ensure the scratch card works on mobile touch screens.

Ensure the countdown is functional.

Ensure every event has its own independent date, time and venue.

Use smooth, premium animations without compromising performance.

The final experience should look like a luxury, interactive Indian wedding invitation card brought to life as a mobile website.
